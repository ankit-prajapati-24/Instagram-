PINNED COMMENT — the single most important field here.
  It must be a real, open question with TWO DEFENSIBLE SIDES, both grounded in
  the script. The reader should be able to pick a side and argue it.
  Shape: state position A. State position B. Ask which one they trust.
  BANNED: "what do you think?", "comment below", "aapka kya khayal hai".
  A generic prompt gets generic engagement and is worth nothing.
