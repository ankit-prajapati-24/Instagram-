Every beat, before the footage is fetched

Listen to each one. To say a beat yourself, copy its line into whatever voice you want to use, then upload what comes back — mp3, wav, m4a, ogg, flac, or the video you recorded on a phone. It is re-encoded to match the rest of the narration and levelled to the same loudness, so an uploaded beat does not jump out next to a synthesised one. Replacing a beat changes how long the video is, which is what the line below tracks.

If the voice simply says a word wrong, you do not need a recording at all: fix the spelling in the spoken line and press Say it again. Only that beat is spoken again. The two lines move independently on purpose — respell the word phonetically in the spoken line and the burned caption keeps the real spelling, so the listener hears it right and the viewer reads it right. Editing only the caption re-times the words without saying anything again.

Sticker art

One card per sticker cue that actually fired in this reel — a beat with no cue has nothing to show here. The candidates under each card come from the terms the script asked for; where it asked for nothing, the built-in word list decides. Five concepts ship with art already committed and the rest fall back to an emoji, so a card with no candidates still renders something. The icon library is named for things rather than ideas, so if the script's terms found nothing useful, search it yourself — “cloud” finds an icon where “dream” finds none. Choosing one bakes it into this reel, which takes about eight seconds.

The library is named for objects, so search the thing that stands for the idea — “dream” finds nothing, “cloud” does. Every result can go on any of the stickers below; choose which one under the icon.

Music bed

One bed for the whole reel, ducked under the voice automatically. Nothing is downloaded until you choose a track, and the length does not matter — a short loop is repeated to fill the video. Only tracks that may be used commercially are offered. Some ask for a credit line in return; the row says which, and the credit is added to the caption for you.
